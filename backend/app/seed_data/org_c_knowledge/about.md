# Everest Outdoor Equipment: About Us

## Who we are
Everest Outdoor Equipment is a Kathmandu supplier of expedition-grade trekking and mountaineering gear. We outfit guides, climbing teams and independent trekkers heading for Himalayan routes between 3,000 and 8,000 metres.

## Our standards
Every item is cold-tested and checked by a working mountain guide before it goes on the shelf. We only stock gear we would take above Camp 2 ourselves.
