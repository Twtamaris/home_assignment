# Everest Outdoor Equipment: FAQ

## Q1: Can I rent gear instead of buying it?
Yes. Ask in store about weekly rental for down suits, crampons and ice axes. A refundable deposit applies.

## Q2: How do I choose the right down suit?
Pick the suit rated for the highest altitude and lowest temperature of your route, then size for layering underneath. Our advisors can fit you in store.

## Q3: Do you repair gear?
We repair zips, seams and baffles on gear bought from us. Repairs usually take 5 to 7 days.
