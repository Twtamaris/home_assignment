# Everest Outdoor Equipment: Shipping and Returns

## Delivery
- Kathmandu Valley delivery takes 1 to 2 business days.
- Delivery to other districts takes 3 to 5 business days.
- Expedition orders above NPR 50,000 ship free within Nepal.

## Cancelling or changing an order
An order can be changed or cancelled free of charge only while its status is `pending`. Once it is confirmed it moves to the warehouse for fitting and packing.

## Returns
Unused gear in its original packaging can be returned within 14 days. Down suits that have been worn or compressed for storage cannot be returned.
