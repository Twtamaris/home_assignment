# Himali Tea House: Product Descriptions

This document describes what each product is, where it comes from and how it tastes. It does not list prices or stock. Those change, so the assistant reads them live from the catalogue each time.

## Himalayan Spring Silver Needle (100g)
- **Category:** White Tea
- **SKU:** HT-SN-100
- **About:** Exquisite hand-picked tender buds from 2200m Illam slopes with delicate notes of honeysuckle and melon.
- **How to brew or care for it:** Brew with 75 to 80 degrees C water; never pour boiling water straight onto the buds.

## Dhankuta Imperial Green Pearls (100g)
- **Category:** Green Tea
- **SKU:** HT-GT-100
- **About:** Tightly rolled emerald pearls uncurling into a fragrant toasted chestnut liquor with fresh vegetative sweetness.
- **How to brew or care for it:** Brew with 80 to 85 degrees C water for about 2 minutes to avoid bitterness.

## Shangri-La High Mountain Oolong (100g)
- **Category:** Oolong Tea
- **SKU:** HT-OL-100
- **About:** Semi-oxidized medium roast oolong revealing honeyed peach, orchid blooms, and a buttery velvet finish.
- **How to brew or care for it:** Brew with 90 to 95 degrees C water after a quick rinse; it opens over many short infusions.

## Golden Tippy Orthodox Black Tea (100g)
- **Category:** Black Tea
- **SKU:** HT-BT-100
- **About:** Abundant golden tips offering a robust, malty body with ripe plum and cocoa undertones.
- **How to brew or care for it:** Brew with 95 to 100 degrees C water for 3.5 to 4 minutes.
