# About Himali Tea House

## Our Story
Founded in 2018 in the tranquil foothills of Illam, Nepal, Himali Tea House is dedicated to bringing single-origin, artisanal Himalayan loose-leaf teas directly from high-altitude slopes to teacups worldwide. Our cooperative works directly with smallholder tea farmers who cultivate tea bushes at elevations ranging between 1,800 and 2,400 meters above sea level.

## Sourcing Philosophy and Direct Trade
We believe in 100% transparent and ethical direct trade. By eliminating unnecessary middlemen, we guarantee that our smallholder growers receive over 40% above fair-trade floor rates. Our processing estates strictly maintain organic cultivation techniques, utilizing natural compost, rainwater collection, and biodynamic plucking schedules aligned with lunar cycles and seasonal flushes.

## Sustainable Packaging
Every package we dispatch uses compostable rice-paper outer pouches, food-grade PLA lining derived from non-GMO plant starches, and airtight reusable aluminum caddies. Our carbon-neutral shipping initiative offsets all ground and air shipping emissions through local re-forestation projects across the eastern hills of Nepal.

## The Tea Master
All Himali blends and single-estate batches are curated and cupped under the supervision of Master Tea Artisan Pema Sherpa, who brings 32 years of orthodox and specialty tea crafting expertise across Darjeeling, Illam, and Dhankuta estates.
