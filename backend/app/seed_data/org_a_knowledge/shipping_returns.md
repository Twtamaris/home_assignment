# Himali Tea House: Shipping, Returns and Refund Policy

## Shipping Coverage & Timelines
- **Inside Kathmandu Valley:** Express delivery is fulfilled within 24 to 48 hours from order confirmation. Same-day delivery is available for orders confirmed before 11:00 AM on business days.
- **Major Nepal Hubs (Pokhara, Chitwan, Biratnagar, Butwal, Dharan):** 2 to 4 business days via courier services.
- **Remote District Deliveries:** 4 to 7 business days depending on road and weather conditions.
- **International Shipping:** DHL Express and Aramex shipping to North America, Europe, Australia, and Asia (5 to 9 business days). Customs duties or local import taxes are the buyer's responsibility.

## Shipping Fees and Free Delivery
- Kathmandu Valley flat delivery rate: NPR 120.
- Out-of-valley domestic flat delivery rate: NPR 250.
- Free Domestic Delivery: Applied automatically to all orders exceeding NPR 2,500 across Nepal.
- International shipping rates start at NPR 3,200 for parcels under 500g and scale by gross weight.

## Order Cancellation Policy
- Orders can be cancelled or edited free of charge exclusively while their status remains `pending`.
- Once an order transitions to `confirmed`, processing in the warehouse begins and cancellation is no longer possible through the chat agent; customers must reach out to manual support.

## Returns and Refund Policy
- Unopened loose-leaf pouches and unused teaware in original packaging may be returned within 14 calendar days of delivery receipt.
- Opened tea pouches cannot be returned or refunded due to food safety and hygiene regulations, unless the product arrived contaminated, degraded, or incorrect.
- If teaware arrives damaged in transit, report the issue with photographic evidence within 48 hours of delivery to qualify for an immediate free replacement or 100% refund.
- Approved refunds are processed to the original payment source (e.g. eSewa, Khalti, Bank Transfer, or card) within 3 to 5 business days. Cash on delivery orders are refunded via local digital wallet or bank transfer.
