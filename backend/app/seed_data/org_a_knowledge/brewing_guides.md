# Himali Tea House: Master Brewing Guides

## 1. Himalayan White Tea (Spring Silver Needle)
- **Water Temperature:** 75°C to 80°C (167°F to 176°F)
- **Leaf-to-Water Ratio:** 3g per 200ml water (Western style) or 4g per 100ml (Gaiwan)
- **Steep Duration:** 3 to 4 minutes (Western) or 30s, 45s, 60s, 90s (Gaiwan)
- **Tasting Profile:** Delicate notes of wild honeysuckle, sweet hay, cucumber, and velvet nectar finish.
- **Tip:** Never use boiling water directly on tender silver needle buds; let boiling water sit for 4 minutes before pouring.

## 2. Himalayan Green Pearl (Single Estate)
- **Water Temperature:** 80°C to 85°C (176°F to 185°F)
- **Leaf-to-Water Ratio:** 2.5g per 200ml water
- **Steep Duration:** 2 minutes. Do not over-steep to prevent bitter tannin extraction.
- **Tasting Profile:** Fresh toasted chestnuts, crisp sweet grass, and gentle mineral minerality.
- **Multiple Infusions:** Excellent for 3 infusions.

## 3. High Mountain Oolong (Shangri-La Estate)
- **Water Temperature:** 90°C to 95°C (194°F to 203°F)
- **Leaf-to-Water Ratio:** 5g per 150ml (Gaiwan) or 3g per 250ml (Mug/Pot)
- **Steep Duration:** Quick rinse (3 seconds, discard), then 25s, 35s, 50s, 70s, 90s.
- **Tasting Profile:** Orchid florals, roasted peach, honeyed butter, and lingering sweet finish.

## 4. Golden Tippy Black Tea (Dhankuta Reserve)
- **Water Temperature:** 95°C to 100°C (203°F to 212°F)
- **Leaf-to-Water Ratio:** 3g per 220ml water
- **Steep Duration:** 3.5 to 4 minutes
- **Tasting Profile:** Ripe dark plum, cocoa nibs, muscatel grape, and malty mountain sweetness. Pairs delightfully plain or with a droplet of organic honey.

## 5. Mount Everest Masala Chai
- **Water & Milk Ratio:** 150ml filtered water + 100ml whole milk (or oat milk)
- **Leaf Quantity:** 5g spiced CTC black tea blend
- **Method:** Simmer water with spices for 3 minutes until aromatic, add tea leaves and simmer for 2 minutes, pour milk and bring to a rolling boil, strain and sweeten with raw cane sugar.
