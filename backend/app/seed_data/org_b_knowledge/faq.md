# Lotus Wellness Studio: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: What time zone does Lotus Wellness Studio operate in?
All our operating hours, practitioner schedules, and appointment times are strictly in the **Asia/Kathmandu (UTC+05:45)** time zone.

### Q2: How far in advance should I book my appointment?
We recommend booking at least 24 to 48 hours in advance, particularly for weekend slots and peak evening sessions between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM.

### Q3: What should I wear for a physiotherapy appointment?
Please wear comfortable, flexible athletic wear such as shorts and a loose t-shirt or tank top. This allows the physiotherapist to observe joint kinematics and access trigger points without impedance.

### Q4: What should I wear for a massage treatment?
During massage sessions, professional draping is used at all times. You may undress to your comfort level (most clients wear studio-provided disposable undergarments or their own underwear). Only the specific body region being worked on is uncovered.

### Q5: Can I request a specific practitioner?
Yes! Our booking agent allows you to select your preferred practitioner (Dr. Sunita Shrestha for physiotherapy and dry needling, Master Tenzing Norbu for massage therapies, or Aarati Maharjan for yoga and sound healing) or choose any available practitioner.

### Q6: Can I book two appointments at the same time?
No. To prevent double-booking and accidental duplicate appointments, a single customer account cannot hold two simultaneous bookings for the same time window.

### Q7: What is your cancellation policy?
You may cancel free of charge if you do so at least 4 hours before your scheduled appointment. Cancellations made within 4 hours of the appointment incur a 50% fee.

### Q8: How does appointment rescheduling work?
You can reschedule any upcoming booking free of charge up to 2 hours before the start time. If the new slot you request is already taken, our booking agent will refuse the conflict and present the nearest alternative free slots.

### Q9: What happens if I arrive late?
If you arrive late, your session will conclude at the scheduled end time so following appointments remain on schedule. The full session price is charged.

### Q10: Are shower facilities available?
Yes, our studio offers private rain shower facilities stocked with organic botanical body washes, hair dryers, clean towels, and secure lockers.

### Q11: Do I need to bring my own yoga mat for 1-on-1 yoga?
We provide premium Manduka natural rubber yoga mats, organic cotton yoga straps, cork blocks, and bolsters. However, you are welcome to bring your personal mat if you prefer.

### Q12: Can pregnant clients book massage therapies?
Yes, after the first trimester (week 13 onwards), our specialized gentle prenatal massage and supportive side-lying cushions are available. Please notify us during booking.

### Q13: Do you offer health insurance reimbursement receipts?
Yes. Dr. Sunita Shrestha is a registered physiotherapist with the Nepal Health Professional Council. We provide itemized clinical invoices with practitioner registration details for insurance claims.

### Q14: What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash on arrival, eSewa, Khalti, Fonepay QR, and major domestic/international credit cards.

### Q15: Are couples or tandem sessions available?
Yes, our Deluxe Lotus Suite accommodates two clients simultaneously for dual massage or sound bath sessions. Please book two adjacent practitioner slots or contact front desk.

### Q16: What holidays is the studio closed on?
Lotus Wellness Studio observes major national festivals including Dashain (Maha Ashtami to Vijaya Dashami), Tihar (Laxmi Puja and Bhai Tika), and Nepali New Year (Baisakh 1). Specific dates are announced in advance and blocked from the booking calendar.
