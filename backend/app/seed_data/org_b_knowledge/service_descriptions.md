# Lotus Wellness Studio: Service Descriptions & Therapies

## 1. Deep Tissue Therapeutic Massage
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Price:** NPR 3,500
- **Overview:** Targeted myofascial release aimed at chronic muscular tension, postural strain, and athletic tightness. Utilizes firm sustained pressure, trigger point manipulation, and warm therapeutic mountain arnica balm. Recommended for desk workers with chronic upper back tension and runners with hamstring tightness.
