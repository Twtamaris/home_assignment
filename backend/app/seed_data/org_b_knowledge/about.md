# About Lotus Wellness Studio

## Our Sanctuary
Situated in the serene green environs of Sanepa, Lalitpur (Kathmandu Valley), Lotus Wellness Studio is an integrative wellness clinic and holistic therapy retreat founded in 2020. Our philosophy harmonizes contemporary evidence-based physiotherapy, time-tested Ayurvedic restorative treatments, and mindful yogic breathwork.

## Atmosphere and Hygiene Protocol
We maintain hospital-grade sanitization across all therapy suites. Every suite features organic cotton linens washed at 60°C with hypoallergenic detergents, pure beeswax candles, custom acoustic sound isolation, and medical-grade HEPA air purifiers. Our relaxation lounges offer herbal infusions and mineral water prior to and following every treatment.

## Studio Hours & Timezone
Lotus Wellness Studio operates in the **Asia/Kathmandu timezone (UTC+05:45)**. All appointment bookings, schedules, and practitioner consultations are strictly anchored to Nepal Standard Time.
- Monday through Friday: 08:00 AM – 07:00 PM
- Saturday: 09:00 AM – 06:00 PM
- Sunday: 10:00 AM – 04:00 PM

## Location and Parking
We are located at Ward 2, Sanepa Heights Road, Lalitpur. Dedicated on-site covered car parking and bike parking spaces are reserved exclusively for studio guests.
