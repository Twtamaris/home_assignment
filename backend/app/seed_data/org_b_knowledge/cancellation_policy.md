# Lotus Wellness Studio: Booking, Rescheduling and Cancellation Policies

## Cancellation Policy
- **Free Cancellation Window:** Cancellations initiated at least **4 hours** before the scheduled appointment start time incur zero cancellation fee and qualify for full deposit credit or complete refund.
- **Late Cancellation (Within 4 Hours):** Cancellations made less than 4 hours prior to appointment time are subject to a **50% late cancellation fee** to compensate reserved practitioner time.
- **No-Show Policy:** Missing an appointment without prior communication results in forfeiture of 100% of the session fee.

## Rescheduling Policy
- **Free Rescheduling:** Customers may reschedule their appointment without penalty up to **2 hours** prior to the original appointment time, subject to practitioner availability.
- **Conflict Handling:** If a requested reschedule slot conflicts with an existing booking or practitioner holiday, the request is refused and our booking agent provides the closest available alternative slots.
- **Maximum Reschedules:** Each appointment may be rescheduled a maximum of 2 times.

## Arrival and Late Arrival Terms
- Guests are kindly requested to arrive **10 to 15 minutes before** their scheduled appointment to complete any health check questions, sip herbal tea, and change into studio robes comfortably.
- If a client arrives late, the session will conclude at the originally scheduled finish time to ensure subsequent appointments are not disrupted. Full treatment fee applies.

## Health Contraindications and Notice
- Please inform our practitioners before treatment if you are pregnant, have acute spinal fractures, deep vein thrombosis, open skin wounds, or contagious fevers. Certain high-intensity deep tissue techniques are contraindicated during early pregnancy.
